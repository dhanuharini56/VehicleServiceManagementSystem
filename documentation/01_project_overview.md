# Project Overview
## Problem Statement
Manual service-center registers make customer, vehicle, appointment, service and billing records difficult to organize, retrieve and analyze. This project provides a centralized system.
## Objectives
1. Maintain customer and vehicle details.
2. Book service appointments.
3. Maintain vehicle service history.
4. Record bills and payment status.
5. Analyze maintenance costs and service activity.
## Scope
The prototype supports registration, appointment, service record, billing and basic reports. Online payment, SMS, authentication and public deployment are future enhancements.
